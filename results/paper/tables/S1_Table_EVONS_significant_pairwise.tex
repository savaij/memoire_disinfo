{\large\bfseries S1 Table. Differences between models on EVONS.}\par\medskip
Each row compares two models on the same prediction task. Of the 55 comparisons, 40 remain significant after the Holm correction: 15 for fake/real labels and 25 for engagement. For engagement, $q$ is the percentile used as the cutoff: for example, .90 means the 90th percentile. $\Delta$F1 is the mean F1 score of model A minus that of model B. Positive values favor A; negative values favor B. Cliff\textquotesingle s $\delta$ follows the same direction. The $p$-values are adjusted across the 15 model pairs for fake/real labels, and separately across the 10 pairs at each engagement cutoff.\par\smallskip
Scores come from ten runs, using the same data splits for both models or text windows in each comparison. In each run, the classification threshold was chosen on a separate calibration set. Only differences with a Holm-adjusted $p<0.05$ are shown. The adjustment includes all comparisons in each group, including those not shown here. The 95\% confidence intervals account approximately for overlap between the data splits; they are not adjusted for multiple comparisons. Cliff's $\delta$ describes how the scores differ across runs; it is not a separate significance test. In the model names, \texttt{bert} means RoBERTa embeddings and \texttt{mistral} means Mistral embeddings.\par\medskip
